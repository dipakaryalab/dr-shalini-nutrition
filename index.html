Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation available online?

Yes. Consultations are available online, so you can work with Dr. Shalini from anywhere. In-person consultations are also available at the clinic in Dwarka, New Delhi.

Do I need to visit the clinic?

No. You can choose an online consultation. If you are in Delhi NCR and prefer to meet in person, you can book a clinic visit instead. Consultation is by appointment only.

How does the nutrition consultation work?

First, we understand your goals, routine, eating habits and relevant health history. Then a nutrition approach is created around your needs and preferences, with follow-up guidance and adjustments as appropriate for your program.

Can I get help with PCOS or women's health?

Yes. Dr. Shalini offers nutrition guidance for PCOS and women's health. Nutrition support works alongside — not in place of — care from your doctor.

Can I get help with weight management?

Yes. The focus is on practical, sustainable eating habits that fit your lifestyle, rather than extreme or one-size-fits-all diets. Individual results vary.

What should I prepare before my consultation?

It helps to have your recent medical reports (if any), a list of current medicines or supplements, your usual daily routine, and a rough idea of what you eat on a typical day.

How personalized is the nutrition plan?

Your plan is built around your goals, health history, daily routine and food preferences, and is reviewed and adjusted during your program as needed.
